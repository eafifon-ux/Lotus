Rythos

Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

Rythos collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you write stays on your own device, and the app sends nothing to any server we control — because there is no such server.

What the app stores, and where

Your notes, folders, photos, settings and calendar preferences are saved in your device’s own browser storage. They are readable only by the app, on that device.

  • We never receive this content. It is not uploaded, backed up or synchronised anywhere.
  • Nobody else can see it unless you export it and share the file yourself.
  • Deleting the app, or clearing its storage, deletes the content permanently. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it for you. Use Export All to keep your own backup.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, Rythos does not collect, store or transmit your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage analytics or crash reports.

Permissions

Rythos asks for no device permissions. If you add a photo to a note, your device shows its own file picker; the app only receives the single image you choose, and that image is stored locally in the note like any other content.

Links that leave the app

Notes can contain links, and the app ships with a small set of shortcuts to common apps and websites. Following any of these takes you out of Rythos. Whatever you open then belongs to that company and is governed by its privacy policy, not this one. We have no involvement in, and no visibility of, what happens after you leave.

Children

Rythos is a general-purpose notebook and calendar. It is not directed at children, and because it collects no data at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins handling data differently, this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the date above will change with it.
